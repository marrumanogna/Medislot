From sign-up to seen, in three steps

MediSlot keeps patients and doctors in separate, purpose-built workspaces.

Create your account

Register as a patient or as a doctor. Doctors add their licence, specialization and clinic details.

Set up or search

Doctors publish weekly availability and consultation types. Patients search by specialization and browse open slots.

Book and manage

Patients book a time that suits them. Doctors see their day at a glance and keep their schedule in order.
